About us

A small travel agency with a personal approach

RIA BENAD TRAVEL LTD is an independent travel agency based in Romford, England. The company was set up in October 2024 and is owned and run by its director, Ria Benad.

Our work is travel agency services: helping you plan a trip and helping you book it. That can mean talking through where to go and when, finding accommodation that fits your budget, arranging hotel stays, helping with flights and transfers, and putting the whole trip together as a clear, tailor-made itinerary.

Because the business is small and owner-run, the help you get is personal and one-to-one. RIA BENAD TRAVEL LTD is registered in England and Wales under company number 16013957.

Services

Help with planning and booking your trip

Four ways we can help, used on their own or together, depending on how much of the trip you'd like us to take on.

Holiday planning

Not sure where to go, or when? We help you weigh up destinations, travel dates and places to stay, starting from your budget and what you enjoy, so the options in front of you actually suit you.

  • Destination ideas matched to your interests
  • Choosing the right dates and trip length
  • Options planned around your budget

Accommodation and travel bookings

Once you know what you want, we take care of the practical side: arranging hotel and accommodation stays and helping you book flights and transfers, so the pieces of your trip fit together.

  • Hotel and accommodation bookings
  • Help finding and booking flights
  • Airport and onward transfers

Tailor-made itineraries

A trip plan built around the people travelling and what they want from it. We put your stays, travel days and ideas for each day into one clear itinerary you can follow.

  • Day-by-day trip plans
  • Multi-stop and combined trips
  • Plans shaped around your pace and interests

Travel advice

Practical guidance before you go: what to check about documents, the best timing for your trip and what to expect when you arrive, so there are fewer surprises along the way.

  • Reminders on passports, visas and entry rules to check
  • Timing tips for seasons and travel days
  • What to expect on arrival
